# Recording and Consent

## What to record

Record your **screen** for the whole scenario, with the wallet extension popups visible. Keep it under 20 minutes per scenario.

Show:

- The page URL and the order id when it appears.
- Every wallet prompt before you sign it (network, amount, destination).
- The final status and the transaction links.

Do **not** show: your seed phrase or secret key (never open them), your email inbox, bank statements with account numbers (blur or crop them), or ID documents.

## How to share

1. Upload the video somewhere that gives a link (Google Drive, YouTube unlisted, Loom).
2. Paste the link in your thread in the **QA feedback** category of this repository's Discussions, with your report.
3. If you do not want to publish a video, say so in the thread: you can still send written notes and screenshots.

## Consent

By taking part you confirm:

1. I use my own wallet and funds, and I understand real money moves on Stellar mainnet during the tests.
2. OfferHub never asked for my seed phrase or secret key.
3. I agree that my report, my recording and the public blockchain transactions from my test may be published by OfferHub (for example on offer-hub.org and in grant reports) to show that independent testers ran the product.
4. I can choose to appear under a nickname. Transaction addresses are public on the blockchain by nature.
5. I may withdraw my recording from publication at any time by writing in my thread; it will be removed from OfferHub's own pages. Copies already shared by others, and the on-chain record, cannot be removed.
6. I am not an employee of or contractor for the OfferHub team, or I say so in my report.

Post **"I agree"** in your first message of the thread to confirm.
